About us

Story of Alitaptap

"Ours is a community within a community. Alitaptap was born out of a shared dream to build an affordable, self-sustaining sanctuary where transient creators can permanently coexist, trade, and exchange knowledge. We treat art not as a luxury for elite galleries, but as a collaborative tool for societal healing, environmental education, and organic grassroots change."

— Henry "Bokeng" Ancanan & Adrian "AJ" Manuel, Founders of Alitaptap Artists Community
