Jebo Padernal

Contemporary Visual Artist and Painter

Amadeo, Cavite

A contemporary visual artist and painter from the Bicol region known for his deeply introspective and raw artistic style.

An alumnus of the Linangan Art Residency, his works primarily utilize oil and soft pastels to explore emotional landscapes, the human form, and the complexities of internal psychology.
